Can one power problem take down your bank?

On Tuesday morning, Google Cloud had a "power event" in one small zone of its London cloud region, europe-west2. Revolut, Lloyds Bank and HSBC confirmed problems, and people also reported trouble with Royal Mail and DPD. More than 1,500 reports about Revolut came in shortly after 08:00 UK time.

One Revolut user told the BBC she could not even pay for parking at a shopping centre. Google says the problem is fixed and will publish an analysis of what went wrong.

The lesson: your app runs on someone else's computers. Ask early "what happens if one zone goes down?" and spread important services across more than one.
