Can a share link leak your private AI chat?

Meta's new AI agent Muse lets people share a chat through a web link. Security firm Claroc found that the chat title went inside the web address itself, like loan-application-draft-a4c9e2.cf.metaaiusercontent.com. Public DNS records save addresses like that, so the researchers found 6,800 shared links. "It took one query," they wrote.

After opening a few hundred, they found things nobody meant to make public: a small-business loan application, a company revenue review, a staff holiday calendar. Meta has paused the sharing feature while it reviews the report.

The lesson: a web address is not a secret. Keep private details out of URLs, and never put them in a subdomain, because DNS records are public.
