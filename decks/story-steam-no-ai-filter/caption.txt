Would you want a "No AI" button on Steam?

Steam Next Fest, Valve's festival for indie games and demos, just started. Players say it is packed with games that use AI-made art or were built almost fully with AI. One Reddit user asked for a filter that hides every game with AI content, since Steam already asks developers to disclose it.

Another player said "it's all AI slop and I dont want to participate." For a filter like that to work, Valve would have to add "AI" as a tag.

The lesson: AI makes shipping a project easy, so shipping alone does not stand out anymore. Care and quality are what people notice.
